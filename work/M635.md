# Colt M635

Spawn class: `mcv_m635`, listed under Submachine Guns.

The user's `MCV_SMD/weapons/v_m635/m635.smd`, updated with the M601 receiver
without a forward assist, is compiled without changing its geometry or rig.
The standard-grip CAR-15 donor has matching bind matrices for
all shared bones and supplies its 30-round animation set. This is separate from
the XM177 SOG donor's vertical-grip poses. The Uzi-derived magazine has a logical
capacity of 32, plus one chambered round after a tactical reload.

Muzzle: VM Base `(-16.5, 0, 1)`, seven units behind the XM177/CAR-15's
`(-23.5, 0, 1)`. The matching world model uses the same mapping; its muzzle is
`(0, 0.7, 14.55)` in weapon_bone coordinates. A compact collision hull is generated
from this mesh, rather than keeping a rifle-length hull.

Firing, draw, holster and reload recordings use the S&W M76 sound set. The
CAR-15 reload motion and duration are retained. The M76 magazine-in sound plays
at frame 29 instead of 44 (0.5 seconds earlier at 30 FPS), on both ordinary and
empty reloads. The ammo display and gameplay reload deadlines are unchanged.
Third-person hold types use the rifle poses for the M16 stock and foregrip.

## Stats

850 RPM (chosen within the documented Colt SMG range), 396 m/s muzzle velocity,
2.61 kg unloaded, 9x19mm / pistol reserve pool, semi and automatic fire.
32 rounds on spawn; 33 after a tactical reload, 32 after an empty reload.

The [Colt catalogue](https://www.colt.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/24-COLT-2263-COLLATERAL-MLE_Catalog-web-FINAL.pdf)
lists roughly 700-1000 RPM for the SMG family. [Dockery Armory's Model 635 reference](https://dockeryarmory.com/colt-model-635-2-2/)
documents 396 m/s, 20/32-round feeding, closed-bolt operation and semi/automatic
selection. These physical figures inform the game's handling; they do not define
game damage. Damage 25, range modifier 0.86, hip/aimed spread 7/2 and reduced view
recoil are balance choices relative to the addon's M76. Material penetration and
other 9mm effects inherit the M76's values.

## Rebuild

`python work/build_m635.py --install` builds and installs the first-person model,
world model and silhouette icon. Requires the existing ported CAR-15/Mk.4 donor
assets and scratch compile game. Build logs are in `work/m635_build/`.

For hand editing, `work/MCV_SMD/weapons/v_m635/` now contains local view/world QCs,
all their source dependencies under `assets/`, and `compile.py`. Edit those files
and run `python compile.py` there, or compile the QCs in Crowbar. This preserves
your edits; the generation script above reconstructs the initial port instead.

`work/tests/m635_assets.txt` checks the model, sights, sounds and reload capacities.
It passed in a freshly started local multiplayer instance: both models loaded,
the iron sights aligned, the M76 sound entries resolved, and reloads produced
33 rounds tactically and 32 from empty. Hip, aimed and world views were inspected.
Prediction regression tests are intentionally omitted at the user's request.
The M601 receiver rebuild was checked again in a fresh local multiplayer game:
both models resolve the added `v_m601/m601` material and render correctly.
`work/check_custom_model_events.py` reads the installed model and verifies the
magazine-in sound at 29/30 seconds in both reload sequences. Results and updated
screenshots are in `work/custom_weapon_validation/`.
**A full Garry's Mod restart is required for the new models.** Lua-only changes
need a map change.
